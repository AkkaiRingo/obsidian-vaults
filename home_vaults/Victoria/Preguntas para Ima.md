## Rage & Spells (V)

> [!tip] [Rage](https://www.dndbeyond.com/classes/2190875-barbarian#BarbarianClassFeatures): 
> **_No Concentration or Spells._** You can’t maintain Concentration, and you can’t cast spells.
> > [!question] Why?
> > Mainly, breaking Concentration becomes really hard:
> > - Barbarians get CON save proficiency
> > - While Raging, some damage is halved, so the CON roll would be very low
>
> I propose:
> > [!example] Solution :3
> > - Multiclass, first lvl won't be Barbarian, so no CON save proficiency
> > - For Concentration rolls, consider the FULL damage to set the CON save DC, not the halved one
